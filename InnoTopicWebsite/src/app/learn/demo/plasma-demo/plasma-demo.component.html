Plasma Demo

@innotopic/plasma-ui

<animated-plasma> renders a classic demoscene-style plasma effect into a full-size WebGL canvas. It's sized entirely via CSS on its host element, and exposes just two knobs: scale (spatial frequency) and speed (playback rate).

Default

Larger swirls, slower (scale=3, speed=0.4)

Busier, faster (scale=12, speed=2.5)
