## Project: LifeSuite

Roles: Founder, Angular/Ionic/Android/iOS Developer

Ionic 5 App (Web, Android, iOS) for finding solutions (psychology, motivation, productivity, etc.) to common life problems and to see life overview in terms of charts (SVG) like Ikigai, Flow State, etc. Released to Google Play and soon to Apple App Store. Has a search engine with Damerau-Levenshtein distance between strings - fuzzy search taking typos into account, with memoization for performance optimization. Strictest settings of TypeScript and Angular.

Technologies: Angular, Ionic, Sass, TypeScript, NgRx, RxJS, Karma, Jasmine, TestCafe, Bash, Bootstrap, Firebase, Cloud Firestore, GCP - Google Cloud Platform, Git, GitHub, Android, Java, Kotlin, Groovy, Gradle, iOS, Google Play, SVG, Gravit Designer, Affinity Designer, Docker, Hexagonal Architecture, LangGraph, LangChain, Amazon Bedrock, OpenAI
